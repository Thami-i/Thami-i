Survey data checker

Paste or load a CSV export from the remote work survey and this tool checks every row against the survey’s rules: required answers, allowed values, number ranges, skip logic, duplicates and formatting. It runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Result

Rules checked

  • The header must contain all ten expected columns. Extra columns are flagged as a warning.
  • Each row must have the same number of cells as the header, which catches an unquoted comma.
  • response_id is a whole number and unique. submitted_at is a valid date.
  • remote_status is required and must be one of the three allowed answers.
  • remote_days and tools are required and valid for “Yes, fully remote” or “Hybrid”, and must be blank otherwise.
  • remote_interest is required for “No, on site” and must be blank otherwise.
  • internet is required, from 1 to 5. contact_ok is required, Yes or No.
  • email is required and valid when contact_ok is Yes, and must be blank when it is No.
  • comments has a 200 character limit. Rows that repeat another row’s answers are flagged as a warning.
